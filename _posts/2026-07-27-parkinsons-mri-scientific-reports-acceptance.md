I am thrilled to announce that the paper titled **"[The practical impact of numerical variability on structural MRI measures of Parkinson's disease](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/early/2026/01/09/2026.01.09.698203.full.pdf)"** has been accepted for publication in *Scientific Reports*.

## Description
This paper quantifies numerical variability in structural MRI measures of Parkinson's disease by instrumenting FreeSurfer to simulate numerical differences across computational environments. It introduces the Numerical-Population Variability Ratio (NPVR) to assess how numerical noise can influence reported group differences and clinical associations.
