**[Numerical Uncertainty in Linear Registration: An Experimental Study](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-06593-3_6)** appears in the UNSURE 2025 proceedings (*Lecture Notes in Computer Science*, vol. 16166, pp. 56–66). The [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00781) is available on arXiv.

<cite-o-meter doi="10.1007/978-3-032-06593-3_6" arxiv="2508.00781" layout="badge" lazy="true"></cite-o-meter>

## Description
This paper studies how numerical uncertainty propagates in linear registration and quantifies its impact on outcomes. The results inform how to interpret variability and improve reliability in neuroimaging registration workflows.
